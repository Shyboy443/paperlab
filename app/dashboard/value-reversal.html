The published signal rule

  1. 60 price rows, equal per-candle volume per touched row, 70% input, source expansion from POC. Developing profiles include the current closed candle; previous-session levels stay fixed.
  2. A close outside arms the setup. Remember the last same-direction breakout volume across intervening opposite/doji candles; flag a decrease.
  3. An opposing body engulf closes inside with more volume than the remembered directional volume. Count outside candles only: five in the video, ten in the published default.
  4. Keep every indicator arrow. Trade only when entry rules permit; never count a displayed arrow as a filled order.

0.5% stop risk, whole shares, exposure capped at wallet equity. 20% drawdown halts a book. Costs: 1 bp fee allowance plus 2 bps slippage per side; stress doubles both.
