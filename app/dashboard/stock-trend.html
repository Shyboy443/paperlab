What this build can reproduce

The universe rotates monthly: S&P 500 members with at least 504 observed trading days, ranked by prior 60-day average dollar volume, with a $10 million liquidity floor. The top 20 form the equal-weight trend index. From them, select five by the previous 63-session return on each month’s first completed session. Selection stays fixed for that month. Signals use completed closes; execution follows at the next regular open.

This is a new five-stock variant. It does not inherit the original twenty-stock strategy’s 23.38% historical CAGR. Current winners are selected from prior observed returns, never from the best full-period backtest outcomes.

Free IEX quotes size trades; Alpaca supplies actual market-order fills. IEX covers a single exchange and is not consolidated SIP. Completed daily signals still use historical adjusted SIP bars. Live execution reserves 1% for cash, uses actual broker fills and interest, and caps capital at the amount you authorize. It does not reproduce the backtest’s opening print or simulated T-bill yield. Daily adjustments below $1 are skipped and reported, so positions can drift from equal weights. Dropped stocks and cash-regime holdings are exited by their actual fractional share quantity. Buys are bounded by the strategy’s own remaining capital; spare account cash cannot refill its losses.

A $9.50 allocation targets about $1.88 in each of five stocks, with about $0.10 left as cash. The five-stock minimum initial allocation is $5.06. Prices, fees, losses and small-order restrictions still affect the actual portfolio.
