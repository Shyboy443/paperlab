# Continuous Binance market research

Runs as an automatically supervised process inside the Railway service. `AUTORESEARCH_ENABLED=true` starts it at every boot. No desktop scheduler is needed for the operating loop. Railway is configured to remain awake and restart after any exit. The worker restarts after crashes and stalls, with bounded backoff; exchange throttling is respected. State lives in `/data/autonomous-research.db` on the existing volume.

Scope: every active Binance **USDT spot pair** and **USDT perpetual contract**. Listings, turnover and spreads refresh every five minutes; per-market hourly observations are retained for 120 days. Other quote currencies, delivery futures, stocks and other exchanges are outside this research program. The public page explicitly reports actual coverage, errors, and history progress.

Deep study rotates through all eligible pairs rather than permanently restricting research to the top few. Eligibility requires 2 million USDT daily turnover and at most a 12-basis-point spread. Ninety days of hourly history are persisted, repaired and updated; forming candles and incomplete UTC four-hour groups are excluded. Delisted/inactive markets receive no new research entries. Paper forward positions continue to be monitored.

Twelve frozen template variants cover trend breakouts, trend pullbacks and flat-regime reversion, with lookbacks 20/40 and stops 1.5/2.5 ATR. Spot is long-only. Every template may abstain. Parameter selection uses only the first 60% of history; the next 20% validates and the final 20% is held out. Entries occur on the subsequent candle, stops take priority when stop and target are both touched, and open positions are closed at each test boundary.

Costs include spot 0.10% or perpetual 0.055% taker fees per side, conservative 10-basis-point historical slippage per side, and recorded perpetual funding. Gates require positive net, profit factor ≥1.2, maximum drawdown ≤10%, ≥20 training trades and ≥10 trades in each later window. The largest holdout winner must represent ≤40% of gross gains; holdout net must remain positive under doubled fees/slippage. Missing history, gaps and funding coverage are explicit rejections. Rates are conservative configured research assumptions, not a fetched personal fee tier. Current exchange quantity steps, minimum quantities and minimum notionals gate every test and forward fill; rejected sizes are recorded in test results. Filters are current-market constraints, not archival filter histories.

Passing history admits at most eight concurrent **paper probation** books, each starting at 1,000 simulated USDT. Books risk 0.5% per trade and cap notional below one times equity. Rules are fixed for each book; new searches cannot rewrite an open book. Forward entry uses a fresh bid/ask after the signal close; old missed signals are skipped. Stops, targets, maximum hold, funding and data errors are monitored continuously. Missed stop touches recovered from closed bars are explicitly marked estimates and use the worse of the stop/gap and current executable side. Trade ledger and book state commit atomically. A drawdown of 10% or equity ≤900 halts that experiment; research remains running.

Forward qualification requires seven elapsed days, thirty closed trades, positive net and profit factor ≥1.2. This is still paper evidence, never authority to send real-money orders. The data client implements only whitelisted public GET endpoints, holds no credentials, and has no router/order methods. Research does not change existing arena books or the operator-armed live mirror.

Repeated searches and overlapping rolling windows can introduce selection bias. Historical qualification is provisional, not a profit promise. Broader families, subhour execution, order-book history, longer archival studies and other venues can be added as separately versioned research protocols.

Observe at `/public/research`; machine-readable read-only state is `/api/public/research`. `AUTORESEARCH_MAX_PAPER_BOTS` can set a 1–12 book cap (default eight). Disable only by explicit configuration; routine worker recovery, scan rotation and paper experiment progression require no manual on/off action.
